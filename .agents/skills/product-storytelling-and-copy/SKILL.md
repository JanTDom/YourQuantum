# Product Storytelling and Strategic UX Copy

This skill governs the voice, tone, and editorial precision of all written content in the product. It eradicates robotic, placeholder-heavy, and clichéd AI writing in favor of sharp, human-centered, and brand-distinct communication.

---

## 1. Core Principles of Senior UX Writing

* **Zero Placeholder Policy:** Never ship "Lorem ipsum", "John Doe", "example@domain.com", or "Item 1, Item 2". Always generate realistic, domain-accurate data that reflects real-world use cases.
* **No Stated Obviousness:** Avoid meaningless titles like "Dashboard", "Profile Settings Form", or "List of Users". Frame headers around user value, status, or actionable insight (e.g., "Active Deployments", "Account & Security Preferences").
* **Action-Oriented Verbs:** Replace generic button labels ("Submit", "Click here", "OK") with concrete outcomes ("Publish Release", "Connect Database", "Generate API Key").

---

## 2. Voice & Tone Spectrum

Adapt tone intentionally to the product's context:

* **Technical / Developer Tools:** Clear, dense, precise, respectful of technical literacy, zero patronizing jargon, highly actionable error messages.
* **Fintech / Enterprise:** Reassuring, authoritative, unambiguous, transparent about status, irreversible actions, and confirmation states.
* **Creative / Lifestyle:** Expressive, witty, inspiring, warm, emphasizing exploration and personal expression.

---

## 3. High-Leverage Copy Touchpoints

* **Empty States (The First Impression):**
  * Never leave empty states as dead ends ("No items found").
  * Formula: Acknowledge reality + State the benefit of populating it + Clear primary call-to-action button or template.
* **Error Messaging (Turn Failure into Trust):**
  * Avoid blaming the user ("You entered an invalid email").
  * State what happened simply + Provide the exact remedy or next step.
  * Example: "That domain isn't reachable right now. Double-check the DNS records or try re-connecting in a few minutes."
* **Microcopy & Cognitive Reassurance:**
  * Alleviate anxiety near critical inputs or payment steps (e.g. "You won't be charged until after your 14-day trial ends" or "We never store raw private keys").
